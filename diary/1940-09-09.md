**Monday 9 September 1940**

Headache most of the morning.

Pike upset milk on the step and stood talking while I wiped it. Wanted to know about ~~Mr Lucas~~. I told him he was a business partner of Arthur’s. He might could have picked up the bottle instead of asking questions. There is a bottle down twice on the milk account, unless I’m mistaken. (must remember to ask him)

Mr Lucas held the bottle while I finished the step. ‘I hope I find you well, Mrs Askew’ again.

Told him about the headache, ~~no reason to, but he waited for an answer.~~ Generally people ask and go straight on to what they want.

Today I also had a little laugh about Arthur. Asked if he used to lose his spectacles at work as well. Apparently he kept a second pair and lost those too. We both remembered him blaming whoever had tidied up. I was always supposed to be the culprit here. Nice to hear somebody laugh about him. Tull puts on such a voice whenever she mentions him.

Asked Mr Lucas his full name when I went to finish the rent book. Maxin Lucas. I thought he’d said Maxine. He wrote it for me, no e in Maxin. Small neat writing. Looked up at him again. I’d already put ~~Mr~~ in the book. Left it.

- Entered yesterday’s payment, up to 8 October.

Tull says I oughtn’t to have put ~~Mr.~~ She thought a Chinese girl had moved in when he arrived yesterday. Probably because of his long hair. Now she tells me. I had been quite sure he was a man, until that name. Pike says a Japanese young man, so that’s no help either.

Fenn called, who hasn’t bothered with me since telling me off about the blackout curtain. Wanted to know who I’d taken in. Let him look at the book. He read the name and seemed satisfied. At least that was quick.

Mr Lucas went out towards the square, then came back for a little. Saw him reading in the garden. From the side I could see what Tull meant. Might have taken him for a woman myself. I shan’t tell her that. He asked whether there was a way round behind the square through the mews. I said yes, if you knew it. He didn’t ask me how. Out again, then back about six with grey dust on his trouser knees. Very particular about asking how I was again. I said better, which I was by then.

Pike and Tull still arguing about he or she. Do wish they’d find something else more important to fight about. I let the potatoes catch and had to scrape the pan. Not enough left worth keeping for tomorrow.

Took my apron off before we went under the stairs. It was filthy. ~~Mr Lucas~~ Maxin on the bottom step, still in his coat. The two upstairs went to the public shelter and Tull talked about whether we should go too, but we stayed. Can’t get comfortable here. My back aches already.

Later. About one, so Tuesday really. A terrific bang and the whole house shook. Kitchen clock stopped. I thought we’d been hit. He asked if I was all right and waited for me to answer before going out. Had to say yes twice.

I was holding on to the banister. Fenn’s whistle outside, lots of people calling.

Going to leave this now, my dear diary.
