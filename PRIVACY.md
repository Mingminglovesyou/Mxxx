# Privacy

*Draft for review by a data-protection adviser before applications open. Square brackets mark decisions still to be made. This notice is written to cover the information the UK Information Commissioner's Office says people must be given when their data is collected.*

**Who is responsible.** Mingxiang Wang, the artist who convenes Mxxx, is the controller of the personal information this project collects. Contact: connecting@mingxiang.art[, postal address or PO Box if one is used for ICO registration].

**What we collect, and why.** When you apply: your chosen name, the languages you work in, your creative practice, what you would like to bring, an optional question or idea, your GitHub username and public activity, and anything you choose to tell us privately. We use it to consider applications and to run the founding circle. We do not ask for your legal name, contact details or any sensitive information (health, religion or beliefs, sexuality, ethnicity, political opinions, criminal matters), and we make no decisions based on such information; if you share it privately because it matters to your work, we will ask your explicit permission before keeping it.

**Legal basis.** Our legitimate interests in running an art project and assembling its founding circle [to be confirmed by a data-protection adviser; consent where you share something with us privately].

**Who else sees it.** Anything you post on GitHub is held by GitHub (GitHub, Inc. in the United States or GitHub B.V. in the Netherlands, whichever acts as controller for you) under GitHub's own privacy statement, is processed in the United States and other countries, and is public. GitHub is a US company and its availability in some territories is restricted by US law. Private messages go to the email provider that hosts mingxiang.art [name the provider]. The founder may use AI tools to read, translate or sort applications, and will say so when it affects a decision; tell us if you would rather we did not use them on anything you send privately.

**How long we keep it.** Declined or withdrawn applications: six months, then deleted from our own systems. Members' details: for as long as you take part, then deleted or reduced to the published credit you chose. Public GitHub content stays public (see below).

**What we can and cannot delete.** If you ask, we will delete your application issue and our private copies, remove any comments we control (including their edit histories), and ask the owners of any forks we know of to do the same. We cannot remove copies others have already made, forks, or search-engine caches, and anything merged into the repository stays in its history. Please weigh this before you post.

**Your rights.** You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, for corrections, for deletion, or object to our use of it; write to connecting@mingxiang.art. We respond within one month (longer only where a request is complex, and we will tell you).

**Complaints.** Write to us first at connecting@mingxiang.art; we acknowledge complaints within 30 days. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or to the data-protection authority where you live.

**Age.** The founding circle is open to adults aged 18 and over. We do not verify age; if we learn that an applicant is under 18 we will close the application and delete it.

**Changes.** This notice is dated 22 October 2026 (draft, pending the adviser's check); earlier versions remain in the Git history.
