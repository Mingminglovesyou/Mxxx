# Moderation log

Blocks, deletions and archived pull requests are recorded here with the date, what was done, and the reason, with names withheld where safety requires. Hidden or locked comments carry their reason on the comment itself. See the [Ethical Covenant](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

**What each tool does.** Hiding a comment keeps it expandable; deleting one leaves an anonymised timeline entry; locking shows a public reason; blocking a user stops them forking and opening issues or pull requests here; interaction limits are temporary and expire after at most six months.
